About David Keefe

A Career Built on Verified Results

David Keefe is the founder and principal of 4-Point Financial, LLC, a Waltham, Massachusetts-based practice serving individuals, families, and business owners throughout Greater Boston. His 32-year track record with New York Life places him among the top 100 producers out of approximately 12,000 agents nationwide, a distinction earned through sustained, verifiable client outcomes.

David holds the Chartered Financial Consultant and Chartered Life Underwriter designations from The American College of Financial Services, and is licensed under FINRA Series 6 and Series 63 as well as a Life and Health Advisor in Massachusetts and more than 20 additional states.

His practice increasingly centers on elder care and long-term care financial planning, an area he considers among the most underaddressed and highest-need in the industry, alongside comprehensive planning and charitable giving guidance.
