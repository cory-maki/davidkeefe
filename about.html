Professional Background

Three Decades With New York Life

David Keefe has spent 32 years as a financial professional with New York Life, a tenure that places him among the top 100 of approximately 12,000 agents nationwide. That standing reflects sustained production at the highest tier of the profession, recognized through his MDRT Top of the Table designation.

Keefe holds the Chartered Financial Consultant and Chartered Life Underwriter designations from The American College of Financial Services. He is registered under FINRA Series 6 and Series 63, and licensed as a Life and Health Advisor in Massachusetts and more than 20 additional states.

Practice Leadership

Founder of 4-Point Financial, LLC

As owner and principal of 4-Point Financial's website and its Waltham, Massachusetts practice, Keefe leads a team of two full-time and two part-time employees serving individuals, families, and business owners throughout Greater Boston.

David has also mentored financial advisors for more than 20 years, coaching a small group of selected advisors each year, work that has shaped how he approaches both new advisors and long-term clients.

Subject-Matter Leadership

Elder Care and Long-Term Care Planning

Long-term care remains one of the most underaddressed areas of personal financial planning, and David Keefe has built part of his practice specifically around it. He serves as a faculty member and past speaker for Massachusetts Continuing Legal Education (MCLE) programs on elder care financial planning topics.

Keefe has also taught long-term care planning through Newton Adult Community Education, extending his work on elder care financial planning directly to the adult children of aging parents, a high-intent and often underserved audience.

Community Involvement

Roots in Watertown, Newton, and Waltham

David Keefe's community ties run deep in Watertown, where he grew up, and extend across Newton and the Charles River area. He is the former President of the Watertown Chamber of Commerce and a current member of the Charles River Chamber of Commerce. He is active on his Facebook page and shares occasional updates through his Reddit account.

Keefe leads the Waltham club of USA 500 Clubs, a member-based organization for senior-level trusted advisors founded in 2013 by Joe Chatham and headquartered in Wellesley, Massachusetts. He is also completing a three-year term on the Board of Directors of the Boston Estate Planning Council, and previously served as Head Coach for Newton Youth Hockey.

Charitable Work & Civic Identity

Pan-Mass Challenge and Community Giving

David Keefe has ridden in the Pan-Mass Challenge more than 10 times, raising over $40,000 for the Jimmy Fund and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, a sustained commitment that reflects the same long-term orientation he brings to client relationships.

He has spoken at the AMC Annual Summit on charitable giving strategies and continues to support the Catholic Schools Foundation's scholarship fundraising efforts. He also posts periodic updates through his Instagram account.
