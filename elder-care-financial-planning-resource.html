David Keefe on Elder Care Financial Planning: A Resource for Families in Newton and Waltham

📅 August 4, 2026
✎ 4-Point Financial Editorial Team
⌚ 5 min read

Adult children searching for guidance on aging parents' finances often find generic advice instead of specific answers. David Keefe, founder of 4-Point Financial, LLC in Waltham, Massachusetts, has spent much of the past decade addressing that gap directly.

Long-term care planning is among the most underaddressed areas of personal financial planning, and it is also one of the highest-intent search categories for families navigating a parent's declining health. David Keefe has positioned part of his practice specifically around this need, drawing on 32 years of experience with New York Life.

A Faculty Role in Elder Care Education

Keefe serves as a faculty member and past speaker for Massachusetts Continuing Legal Education (MCLE) programs focused on elder care financial planning. The role places him alongside attorneys and other professionals addressing the legal and financial dimensions of aging.

He has also taught long-term care planning through Newton Adult Community Education, a setting that puts him in direct contact with the adult children of aging parents throughout Newton and the surrounding Charles River communities.

Long-term care is among the most underaddressed areas of personal financial planning, and informed guidance remains scarce for the families who need it most.

Why the Audience Matters

The families David Keefe works with in this area are typically not first-time financial planning clients. They are often adult children managing a parent's affairs for the first time, searching for a Waltham or Newton advisor who understands both the financial mechanics of long-term care and the decisions involved.

That distinction shapes how Keefe structures these engagements, with the ChFC and CLU designations he holds providing the technical foundation behind the conversation.

Part of a Broader Practice

Elder care and long-term care planning sit alongside the broader work of 4-Point Financial's practice, which David Keefe founded and leads with a team of two full-time and two part-time staff, serving individuals, families, and business owners across Greater Boston.

Keefe's 32-year track record with New York Life, which places him in the top 100 of approximately 12,000 agents nationwide, underpins the elder care work in the same way it underpins every other part of his practice.

Conclusion

For families in Newton, Waltham, and Watertown facing long-term care decisions, David Keefe's combination of credential-backed expertise and direct teaching experience, alongside his full professional background, offers a specific, checkable resource.
